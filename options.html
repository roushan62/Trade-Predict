Panel defaults

Alt+Shift+A shows or hides the panel on any page. Change the shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Privacy & permissions

  • Capture — the screenshot is taken with chrome.tabs.captureVisibleTab only when you press Analyze or Live Mode fires.
  • Network — the image and prompt go straight to the provider you selected. No analytics, no third-party servers, no telemetry.
  • Keys — kept in local extension storage and injected into the HTTPS request header of that one provider.
  • Drawing — results stay in the tab; Clear removes them instantly.

AI prediction is not financial advice. Trading involves risk.
